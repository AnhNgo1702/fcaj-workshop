# Harvest Report "Commendation Event and Experience Sharing on Participating in the AWS Hackathon by AWS Vietnam Teams"

### Purpose of the Event

- Commend the teams that participated in the AWS Hackathon
- Share experiences about participating in the Hackathon from the involved teams.
- Meet and interact with members of the AWS Vietnam team.

### Highlights

#### Summary and commendation of teams participating in the AWS Hackathon

- Commend the teams that participated in the AWS Hackathon.
- Share experiences about participating in the Hackathon from the involved teams.

#### Diversity of participating teams

- A diverse range of teams from various universities, with many different projects, from AI and machine learning to IoT, blockchain, games, web, and mobile apps.
- There were experienced teams as well as fresh student teams, creating diversity and richness for the competition.

#### Different approaches when facing competition rules

- Experienced teams:
    + Very calm and worked in an organized manner
    + Individuals had worked together before so there were no arguments during work
    + Good demo ideas, perfectly prepared slides

- Student teams:
    + Often anxious and panicked when facing competition rules
    + Arguments during teamwork, high individual egos
    + No prior experience with the competition
    + However, they showed great creativity and ability to adapt well to the environment
    + Creative and interesting ideas, though slides were not yet optimized

### Personal experience when attending the event

- Most participating teams were very well prepared regarding ideas and technology
- The teams shared very detailed information about their workflow, the challenges they faced, and how they solved them
- I learned a lot from the experiences of AWS Vietnam teams participating in the Hackathon
- Both experienced and inexperienced teams faced similar challenges; no one is perfect, what matters is how you face them
- I realized that preparation is very important, not only in terms of ideas and technology but also psychologically and regarding teamwork skills.

### Personal Aspirations

I was truly impressed by the competition; the atmosphere was fantastic, and everyone was very energetic and creative. I hope that in the future I will have the opportunity to participate in a Hackathon.

### Lessons learned from the event

* Need thorough preparation in terms of ideas, technology, and psychology
* Teamwork skills are crucial
* No one is perfect, what matters is how you face difficulties
* Should participate in more activities to hone soft skills
