### Highlights

#### Providing quiz sets with multiple questions of varying difficulty levels

- Questions on AWS theoretical knowledge
- Questions on AWS practical knowledge
- Questions on advanced AWS knowledge
- Each question has a different point value based on its difficulty and detail.
- Each team's score is based on the number of correct answers and the point value of the questions.

#### Game format featuring both tactical elements and entertaining interaction

- Each team has different privileges such as the star of hope or safety question.
- The scoring mechanism includes point deductions, creating many exciting comebacks.
- Even if far ahead, you could still lose just because the final question has high value.
- The questions are practical and highly applicable to work.

#### Professional Hosting

- Beautiful UI, stable performance
- The MCs have good knowledge and English proficiency
- Create a great atmosphere
- Questions in English are translated directly, helping us to proactively improve our English reading comprehension
