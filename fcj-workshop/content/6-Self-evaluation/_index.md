My internship at Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam (June 11, 2026 - August 15, 2026) was a significant milestone that allowed me to step beyond theoretical knowledge and immerse myself in a real-world enterprise environment. This journey not only familiarized me with a professional workplace culture but also fostered my comprehensive personal development.

Below are the core values and skill sets I have cultivated during this period:

- **Mastering Cloud Infrastructure & Core Technologies:** Gained hands-on experience in designing and deploying practical application architectures on AWS. I successfully leveraged core services like virtual servers (EC2), scalable storage (S3), domain routing (Route 53), alongside monitoring tools like CloudWatch and SNS. Additionally, I became proficient in containerizing with Docker, configuring Nginx Reverse Proxy, and integrating AI/OCR solutions into practical workflows.
- **Cultivating a "Growth Mindset" & Problem-Solving Capabilities:** Guided by Amazon's signature "Day 1" philosophy, I learned to maintain curiosity and perseverance when facing complex challenges. Overcoming real-world technical hurdles—such as optimizing memory with Swap files to prevent OOM errors or troubleshooting SSL certificates—has significantly sharpened my analytical and troubleshooting skills.
- **Enhancing Emotional Intelligence (EQ) & Professionalism:** Working in a multinational environment taught me the art of empathetic communication—putting myself in the shoes of users and managers to grasp core requirements. I have become more confident in expanding my professional network, overcoming the psychological barrier of the "fear of making mistakes", and taking proactive ownership of my responsibilities.
