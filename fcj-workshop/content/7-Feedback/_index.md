### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment & Facilities**  
The working environment at the AWS office is truly professional and open. The premium and well-equipped workspace constantly provided me with inspiration and high concentration. I particularly appreciated the small but excellent experiences, such as never having to wait more than 7 minutes for the elevator. My colleagues were incredibly enthusiastic and always willing to help, even outside of working hours.

**2. Support from Mentor & Team Admin**  
Throughout the internship, my Mentor provided highly detailed guidance and thoroughly explained complex technical issues. One thing I highly appreciate is that they always encouraged me to analyze and solve problems independently rather than simply handing me the answers. The Admin team was also extremely supportive regarding procedures and documentation, creating the best conditions for interns to integrate smoothly.

**3. Development Opportunities & Academic Relevance**  
The assigned tasks not only aligned perfectly with what I learned at university but also expanded my knowledge into new areas of Cloud Computing. Working hands-on with the AWS ecosystem helped me solidify a strong foundation and accumulate valuable skills, from project management to professional communication. The intimate sharing sessions and real-world experiences from senior colleagues gave me a much clearer direction for my career path.

**4. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture here is deeply rooted in respect and positivity: everyone works seriously but maintains a fun and sociable atmosphere. Regardless of one's position, whenever a project requires urgency, the entire team steps up to support each other. This made me feel like a true part of the organization.

### Additional Questions

**What were you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
There are too many wonderful things to choose just one. I was deeply impressed by the top-tier workspace in Ho Chi Minh City and the open internal culture. Especially during Events, hearing authentic career stories and life experiences shared by senior staff was incredibly inspiring and insightful.

**What do you think the company could improve for future interns?**  
The working experience was generally perfect, with just one minor operational inconvenience: accessing the restroom area was quite troublesome since hundreds of students had to share a single access card. I suggest the company consider either leaving that door unlocked during office hours or providing more access cards for better convenience.

**Would you recommend your friends to intern here?**  
Absolutely! This is a fantastic opportunity to experience a world-class workspace in HCMC, network with industry experts, and gain invaluable practical knowledge.
